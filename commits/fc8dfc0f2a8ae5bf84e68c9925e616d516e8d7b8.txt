fix(elite): analytics commands read the remote store when a client is connected
EliteRuntime.currentEvents() only returned the live store when a local
watcher was running, so with a streaming client (the primary client-server
mode) it fell through to snapshot() and reported "not configured". It now
checks remoteMachineId first, mirroring summary(), currentSnapshot() and
query(). A bridge test pins that currentEvents() reports source "remote"
and the remote events while a client is connected.
