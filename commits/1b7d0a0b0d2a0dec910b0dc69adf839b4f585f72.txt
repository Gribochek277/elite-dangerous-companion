fix(elite): apply the idle gate to digests, silence delivery in quiet hours, suppress replay notifications
Review round 1 found three blocking issues. This commit fixes them:

- sendOptions now queues non-critical (digest/live) deliveries as a follow-up
  while the agent is busy, so a digest never steers/interrupts a running turn.
- flush() consults quiet hours and holds a queued digest until the window
  closes instead of delivering inside it.
- backfill and startup-bootstrap replays are never notified: ingest carries
  body.mode through onJournalLines, the local watcher marks its bootstrap poll
  in JournalEventsMeta, and notifyEligible also archives any event older than
  the digestMinutes window. Added end-to-end runtime tests and pure
  isNotifiableEvent coverage.
