fix(elite): archive prefix invariant and from-0 backfill refill
The archive writer's byte-size dedup was only sound when the file was a
byte-for-byte prefix of its journal, but a cursor-trimmed first write (cursor
ahead of a fresh or hand-deleted archive) created a tail-only file, and a
cursor reset then appended the replayed head after the tail — missing head,
duplicated tail.

- archive writer now refuses to write a chunk that starts past the archived
  prefix (fromOffset > size) and reports it as gapped; a from-0 replay is the
  only way to fill it, which overlap dedup then handles byte-identically
- ingest passes the raw (untrimmed) chunks to the archive hook, so a from-0
  elite_backfill refills a gapped archive even when the cursor already covers
  the range; the writer's overlap dedup keeps replayed bytes out
- the runtime counts and logs archive gaps distinctly
- tests: nonzero-offset first append is gapped, cursor-reset replay rebuilds
  byte-identically, re-backfill of a consumed range does not duplicate, and a
  cursor-covered elite_backfill refills a gapped archive
