fix(elite): survive post-connect poll failures and enforce the backfill window on every pass
- run() guards its post-connect poll (and uses an interruptible sleep), so a
  transient /ingest failure after a successful connect marks the client
  disconnected and keeps spooling instead of exiting.
- backfill() applies the backfillDays cutoff on every pass, not only the
  first, so a heartbeat-triggered re-backfill never re-uploads old journals.
